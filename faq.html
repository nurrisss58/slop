Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking and travel.

Can I change my flight?

In this prototype, flight changes are represented as a demo interaction. A real service would connect this action to its reservation system.

Can I choose a seat?

Seat selection can be added during booking. The prototype keeps the booking flow intentionally simple.

What payment methods are supported?

The current website is a front-end prototype and does not process real payments.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact page or the support details in the footer.
